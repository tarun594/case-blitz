Terms and Conditions

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Please read these Terms and Conditions carefully before applying for a loan with Ascend. By ticking the box on the application form and submitting it, you confirm that you have read, understood and agreed to them.

1. About these terms

These terms apply to your use of the Ascend website and to every loan request, loan and account created through it. "Ascend", "we" and "us" mean the Ascend project. "You" means the person applying for or holding a loan.

2. Who can apply

  • You must be between 18 and 24 years old (both ages included) and a resident of India.
  • Ascend loans are meant for people who have no earlier credit history. Having no history will not count against you.
  • You must give your own true Aadhar number, PAN number, mobile number, email address and bank account details.
  • Only one active request or loan is allowed for the same identity details at a time (see section 10).

3. Information you give us

  • All information in the application form must be correct and complete to the best of your knowledge.
  • If any information is found to be false, incorrect or misleading at any time, we may reject your request, stop further withdrawals and ask for the dues to be paid.
  • You must tell us about any change in your contact details while a loan is running.
  • Details of a parent or guardian must be given with that person's knowledge.

4. How we decide on your request

  • After you submit the loan request, we calculate an eligibility score out of 100. It looks at the amount asked compared with your income, the size of the amount, special academic achievements (such as JEE, NEET, GATE, CAT or Olympiads) and your Class 10 and Class 12 marks.
  • A score of 75 or more is accepted automatically. A score below 50 is rejected automatically. A score from 50 to 74 is reviewed by our team, who may accept or reject it.
  • Ascend may decline any request, and is not required to give a detailed reason, except where the law requires us to.
  • You will be told the decision by email and on your login page. A Loan Apply ID is created for every request and is needed to log in.

5. Loan amount, interest and repayment

  • The loan amount can be between ₹5,000 and ₹2,00,000, repaid over 6, 12, 18, 24 or 36 months.
  • The yearly interest rate is shown on the loan plan you choose. The sample plans are 12%, 13% and 14% a year.
  • An approved loan is given in stages. Your first withdrawal can be up to ₹1,000. The amount you can withdraw each time grows as you repay on time and has an upper limit of ₹10,000 per stage. It can also reduce if you pay late.
  • Interest is charged on each withdrawal as simple (flat) interest for the repayment period you chose. The total is divided into equal monthly installments, and one installment falls due every 30 days after the withdrawal.
  • You can pay any amount at any time. Money paid is first used for the earliest installment due.
  • You can withdraw a new amount only if it keeps what you owe within your current stage limit and within the approved loan amount.

6. Charges

  • The only charges are the interest shown to you and the late payment penalty in section 7.
  • We do not charge hidden fees. There is no processing fee, prepayment fee or account fee at present. If this ever changes, you will be told before you take a new withdrawal.

7. Late payment

  • If an installment is not paid by its due date, a penalty of 1% of the unpaid amount is added for every day it stays unpaid. The penalty is compounded daily.
  • The penalty stops when the installment is paid in full.
  • Missed installments also reduce your Ascend credit score (section 8) and can reduce the amount you may withdraw in future.

8. Ascend credit score

  • Every borrower starts with a score of 100. Paying installments on time does not change it.
  • The score falls by 5 points when an installment is missed, and by another 5 points for each further 30 days that overdue dues remain unpaid.
  • The score rises by 2 points when you clear an overdue installment along with its penalty, and by 3 points for each later installment you pay, up to a maximum of 100.
  • If the score falls below 90 we will show you a warning. If it falls below 85, no new money will be given until all dues, including interest and penalties, are paid.
  • This is Ascend's own score for managing your loan. It is not a score from a credit bureau. [Edit this point if you will report repayments to a credit bureau.]

9. Using your account safely

  • You log in with your Loan Apply ID and a one-time password (OTP) sent to you.
  • Keep your Loan ID and OTP private. Ascend will never ask you for your OTP by phone, message or email.
  • You are responsible for activity on your account until you tell us it has been misused.

10. One loan at a time

You cannot apply for a new loan while an earlier request is still pending, or while you still owe money on an earlier loan, using the same email, mobile number, Aadhar number, PAN number or account number. A new request can be made once the earlier loan is fully repaid or was rejected.

11. Your data and privacy

  • We collect the details you enter in the form, your reviews and your loan activity so that we can check your request, run your loan and contact you.
  • We do not misuse your data. We do not sell your data or use it for anything unrelated to your loan.
  • We may share it only with banks and payment partners needed to give or collect your loan, with service providers who work for us under a duty of confidentiality, and with authorities when the law requires.
  • We keep your data for as long as your loan is active and afterwards for the period required by law.
  • You may ask us to show, correct or delete your data by writing to the email address in section 15. Some data cannot be deleted while a loan is active or while the law requires us to keep it.
  • Reviews you post, along with the name you enter, can be seen by other visitors.

12. How we treat you

  • We will not harass, threaten or shame you or your family or guardians. We will contact you only in a polite way and at reasonable hours.
  • We will not contact people in your phone contacts about your loan.
  • If you are facing difficulty in repaying, please write to us early so that we can discuss options.

13. What you must not do

  • Give false documents or details, or use another person's identity.
  • Create several requests using changed details to get around the one-loan rule.
  • Try to break into, damage or misuse the website, or post abusive or false reviews.

If you do any of these, we may reject or stop your loan, ask for immediate repayment of dues and report the matter to the authorities.

14. Stopping or changing a loan

  • We may stop new withdrawals at any time if you break these terms, if the information you gave proves wrong, or if the credit score rule in section 8 applies.
  • Amounts you already owe stay payable even if withdrawals are stopped.
  • You may stop using the loan at any time by repaying what you owe.

17. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The date at the top shows when they were last changed. A change will not alter the interest or installments of a withdrawal you have already taken.
